Ministro FELIX FISCHER, DJe 04/12/2020). No mesmo sentido, dentre outros: HC 632.019/SP, Rel. Ministro FELIX FISCHER, DJe 10/2/2021; HC 629/981/SP, Rel. Ministro JOEL ILAN PACIORNIK, DJe 9/2/2021; HC 620.531/SP, Ministro SEBASTIÃO REIS JÚNIOR, DJe 3/2/2021.<br>2.
